## Maintainer hardware observations

**S3, no microphone or microSD adapter:** local candidate OTA succeeded, 8 MiB
PSRAM remained available and selected original audio, CPU and other checked
settings were preserved. Existing board/microphone choices stayed manual and
noise reduction stayed Off after updating. Auto board and microphone choices
were tested independently: no false microphone or AtomS3U identification occurred,
and the previous format and gain were retained. A 30-second observation with Listen
transport passed; this qualifies web/memory/transport with missing peripherals,
not actual audio quality or SD recording.

Actual device UI passed in both themes at 320/390/1280 px, including orange drafts,
poll/revert/discard and no settings writes. Its served embedded page matches the
candidate asset. Noise reduction allocated its engine in PSRAM. In the short
missing-microphone workload it used about 18% of audio time at 160 MHz and 36%
at 80 MHz, without automatically changing the selected frequency. This does not
qualify actual PDM capture, concurrent SD work or all 80 MHz workloads. Original
manual choices, tuned gain, CPU frequency and noise Off were restored after testing.

**C6 with microphone:** candidate build passed; physical candidate OTA, detection,
Listen and filter observation remain pending device availability. Preserve External
antenna when continuing these tests.

## Remaining qualifications

C3/C5, Sense and AtomS3U physical qualification remains pending. No actual SD
recording or battery-runtime qualification was performed for the new filter.
Synthetic DSP tests and quieter audio do not establish better BirdNET recognition;
compare real field recordings with/without filtering, including faint calls,
continuous songs, wind and rain, before recommending it as a default.


## Detection status UI refinement

Audio and Device configuration each show the effective board and microphone
format directly below the relevant selector. Successful detection is distinguished
from a retained fallback or manual choice. Results always describe the confirmed
runtime configuration, including while an unsaved selection is pending. The two
pages share one formatter. Status metadata is restored on failed audio changes.
Source and embedded browser cases cover confirmed, manual and missing-microphone
states in both themes and mobile/desktop widths. No firmware version change or
publication was made for this candidate refinement. C6 physical tests remain deferred.

The refined status rows were verified on the actual S3 in Audio and Device
configuration at 320/390/1280 px in both themes. Drafts did not alter the reported
runtime result; no settings writes were made by those browser checks. Current
Auto choices and other checked settings survived the local OTA, and the served
embedded page matches the candidate. A further 30-second missing-peripheral
web/memory/Listen observation passed; this is not microphone or SD qualification.
