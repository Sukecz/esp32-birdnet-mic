## Maintainer hardware observations

**S3, no microphone or microSD adapter:** local candidate OTA succeeded, 8 MiB
PSRAM remained available and selected original audio, CPU and other checked
settings were preserved. Existing board/microphone choices stayed manual and
noise reduction stayed Off after updating. Auto board and microphone choices
were tested independently: no false microphone or AtomS3U identification occurred,
and the previous format and gain were retained. A 30-second observation with Listen
transport passed; this qualifies web/memory/transport with missing peripherals,
not actual audio quality or SD recording.

Actual device UI passed in both themes at 320/390/1280 px, including orange drafts,
poll/revert/discard and no settings writes. Its served embedded page matches the
candidate asset. Noise reduction allocated its engine in PSRAM. In the short
missing-microphone workload it used about 18% of audio time at 160 MHz and 36%
at 80 MHz, without automatically changing the selected frequency. This does not
qualify actual PDM capture, concurrent SD work or all 80 MHz workloads. Original
manual choices, tuned gain, CPU frequency and noise Off were restored after testing.

**C6 with microphone:** local candidate OTA succeeded. Selected audio, CPU,
streaming/sleep schedule and other checked settings were preserved, including the
External antenna. Capture and actual NTP/UTC/local-offset checks passed. A
30-second RTSP PCM capture and concurrent Listen/memory observation passed.
Auto correctly detected Philips I2S while retaining tuned gain. Actual Audio and
Device configuration UI passed at 320/390/1280 px in both themes, including the
confirmed detection label and drafts that do not overwrite the runtime result.

With noise reduction enabled at 160 MHz, a further 30-second RTSP + Listen
observation passed without increasing capture, DMA or RTSP queue-error counters.
The filter used internal RAM and about 22% of audio time on average. This is a
short observation, not extended stability or BirdNET detection qualification.
The reported internal heap minimum since boot reached about 49 KiB during the
filter workload, so further Wi-Fi/workload qualification remains important.

The same concurrent workload at 80 MHz did not pass the streaming queue check.
Do not recommend C6 noise reduction at 80 MHz for this workload; use 160 MHz.
This does not establish a universal CPU threshold for other boards or modes.
Original manual microphone selection, gain, 160 MHz, streaming/sleep schedule,
External antenna and noise Off were restored after testing. A subsequent
30-second RTSP + Listen observation passed without further queue drops, and
the final capture/NTP check passed.

## Remaining qualifications

C3/C5, Sense and AtomS3U physical qualification remains pending. No actual SD
recording or battery-runtime qualification was performed for the new filter.
Synthetic DSP tests and quieter audio do not establish better BirdNET recognition;
compare real field recordings with/without filtering, including faint calls,
continuous songs, wind and rain, before recommending it as a default.


## Detection status UI refinement

Audio and Device configuration each show the effective board and microphone
format directly below the relevant selector. Successful detection is distinguished
from a retained fallback or manual choice. Results always describe the confirmed
runtime configuration, including while an unsaved selection is pending. The two
pages share one formatter. Status metadata is restored on failed audio changes.
Source and embedded browser cases cover confirmed, manual and missing-microphone
states in both themes and mobile/desktop widths. No firmware version change or
publication was made for this candidate refinement. C6 microphone observations are recorded above; recording and battery qualification remain separate.

The refined status rows were verified on the actual S3 in Audio and Device
configuration at 320/390/1280 px in both themes. Drafts did not alter the reported
runtime result; no settings writes were made by those browser checks. Current
Auto choices and other checked settings survived the local OTA, and the served
embedded page matches the candidate. A further 30-second missing-peripheral
web/memory/Listen observation passed; this is not microphone or SD qualification.


## Noise reduction usability refinement

Save noise reduction is directly below On/Off in the same value cell. Main help
uses shorter practical text; strength/background options and the graph/technical
diagnostics are under separate disclosures. Closed diagnostics do not poll spectra.
Recording-busy feedback appears only when needed. Noise reduction settings retain
the shared draft/save/discard lifecycle and never change High-pass settings.
High-pass reduces low rumble; adaptive reduction addresses steady hiss/hum and
both remain independently configurable. Firmware version remains 2.06.

The revised embedded UI was installed locally on C6 and checked in an actual
browser at 320/390/1280 px in light and dark themes. Saving Off and then restoring
the original On setting through the UI preserved High-pass and the other audio
settings. Draft/revert, polling preservation and discard passed. Auto microphone
selection, External antenna and the checked configuration survived OTA. Capture,
NTP and UTC/local-offset checks passed after upload. All six build variants,
20 host checks, 25 browser checks and the release artifact checks passed; packaged
binaries match the verified source-bound builds. This refinement was not uploaded
to S3 or published.
