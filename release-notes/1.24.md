# Firmware 1.24

The microphone can now keep local time and stream schedules correct when daylight saving time changes.

- Choose your location in **Time & Network**. Auckland / Wellington automatically follows New Zealand standard and summer time; presets also cover Central Europe, the UK, major US zones, and Sydney / Melbourne.
- Manual UTC offsets remain available for other locations, including quarter-hour offsets. An optional custom rule supports places with different annual daylight-saving dates.
- Scheduled streaming and deep-sleep wake times use the selected location. Existing devices keep their current manual offset until you choose a location.

Firmware builds for XIAO ESP32-C3, S3, C5, and C6 passed local validation. The C6 test device synchronized its clock and streamed with the Prague preset. Physical checks on the exact daylight-saving changeover dates and long-run testing are still pending.
