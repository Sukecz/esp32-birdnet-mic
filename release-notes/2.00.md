# BirdNETMic 2.00

**Stream live. Record anywhere.** BirdNETMic grows from a network microphone into
an optional standalone field recorder. Choose Streaming for BirdNET-Go/BirdNET-Pi,
or Recording to collect WAV audio on microSD without a router or computer.

- Timed or continuous recording, split WAV files, power-on start and Stop & save.
- Standalone Device Wi-Fi for setup in the field; switch Wi-Fi off to reduce power,
  with automatic sleep at the end of the offline session. Reset/power-cycle to reconnect.
- Battery voltage/calibration and optional low-battery save/sleep protection.
- New device setup, protected direct access, clearer mode selection and SD/device logs.
- C5 + PDM recommended for new builds; add Adafruit BFF #5683 for SD. Sense uses its
  expansion-board slot but remains experimental and physically untested.

The C5 overnight test covered 9 h 6 min with ten finalized WAV files and no observed
recording drops or new I2S/DMA errors. Battery runtime figures are estimates from
USB power measurements, not multi-day battery test results. Other boards' SD paths
still need physical qualification. Streaming and SD Recording are separate modes.
Bird identification remains a job for a receiving computer or later file analysis.

## Upgrade

Saved settings are retained through normal OTA. Choose the startup mode in Device
configuration; auto-start recording is a separate option. Older devices first take
the permanent v1.35 bridge; C3/C6 may require Prepare device before the next update.
Use only the matching board's app-only image for OTA. S3 covers Sense and AtomS3U;
AtomS3U remains Streaming-only.
