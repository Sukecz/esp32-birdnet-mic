# BirdNETMic 2.03 — Recording and Listen reliability

Recording can start with a PSRAM buffer under the lower internal-memory conditions
found with Device Wi-Fi, PDM and mDNS enabled. Capture restarts use the Recording
working budget, while internal-only SD buffers keep their larger reserve. Actual
allocation failures and battery, thermal or microphone blockers are shown directly.

Listen reports busy connections correctly in Chrome and briefly retries a quick
Stop → Listen restart. Temporary TCP backpressure can recover without dropping the
connection. If the audio transfer ends or stops delivering data, the browser opens
a fresh transfer automatically, preserving playback volume and discarding stale
queued sound. Consecutive failures are bounded, and Stop cancels recovery immediately.
The independent SD recording is unaffected by preview recovery.

Choose your own device Wi-Fi password in Network or Device configuration, using
8–63 printable ASCII characters. Existing passwords and saved settings are retained.
Saved mDNS OFF also stays off when the OTA service starts after a reboot.

See [validation and hardware limits](2.03-validation.md).
